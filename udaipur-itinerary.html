Where to sleep

Book 2–3 rooms, not 8 beds

October is peak season, so book at least 2 weeks out. Split as 2–4 people per room to bring the per-head cost down further.

Hostel dorms / group rooms

  • Zostel Udaipur — dorm beds, walkable to the old city ₹350–600/bed
  • Moustache Udaipur — similar setup, good common areas to hang out as a group ₹400–600/bed
  • Backpacker Panda — budget dorms, quieter option ₹350–550/bed

Guesthouses near the ghats

  • Family-run guesthouses near Hanuman Ghat / Lal Ghat — split a lake-facing room 2–3 ways ₹800–1,500/room
  • Skip anything with "Palace" or "Heritage" in the name right on Lake Pichola's main strip — same view, 3x the price a street back

Before you leave home

Six ways to keep this cheap

  • ✓Book hostel dorms or shared rooms as a group, in advance — October is peak season.
  • ✓Split scooty rentals two-per-bike instead of taking autos everywhere.
  • ✓Skip the paid City Palace museum interior if funds are tight — the courtyards and lake views outside are free and still stunning.
  • ✓Eat where the lanes get narrower, not where the view gets fancier.
  • ✓Carry a bottle and refill instead of buying water every stop.
  • ✓Bargain at markets — open at half the quoted price and settle from there.
